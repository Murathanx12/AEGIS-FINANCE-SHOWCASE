Historical highlights.
Explicit boundaries.

The showcase presents three positive historical examples chosen retrospectively from 307 benchmark-comparable paper accounts. It is a curated project presentation, not a complete account history or current performance report. The selected end date is 9 October 2026; these results were available in the receipt generated on 10 October at 23:52:31 UTC.

Which observations are shown?

Revision flow: simulator, 28 September–9 October, 10 sessions, +10.599% against SPY+1.330%. Net raises: simulator over the same window, +9.759% against SPY+1.330%. Hack2: broker-paper observation, 28 August–9 October, +5.517% against SPY+1.219%; actual series available for 10 of 30 sessions. Exact realized fills are not established here.

These three examples share an overlapping holdings cluster and do not provide independent corroboration. The same-window benchmark must remain with each figure. Excess return is the difference in percentage points. Different windows and overlapping strategies are not one combined portfolio or independent tests. Simulator observations are not evidence of broker execution; broker paper is not live-money performance.

What the selection does not establish

These examples were selected after seeing the results, not pre-registered as a portfolio. They are not a representation of every account, all periods, current results, independently audited returns or repeatable investment skill. No maximum drawdown or cost-inclusive audited performance is supplied by this public extract.

The public JSON contains the exact selected observations, dates, evidence labels, source paths and SHA-256 hashes. The original receipts remain private. Their hashes identify source bytes but cannot independently verify unavailable source records.
