From reading
to reasoning.

Market information becomes useful when the original source, the interpretation and the eventual outcome remain connected.

Aegis combines a research interface with a quantitative experimentation engine. It collects market and company information, retains source references, evaluates hypotheses and records paper outcomes against comparable benchmarks.

Optimus supplies the persistent knowledge layer. Reusable instructions and source-linked findings help the next agent session recover context without relying on a long chat history.

An engineering project
in the open.

A curated view of a larger private implementation.

The inspected implementation contains approximately 520,000 physical lines across the selected production source roots. The stack includes Python, FastAPI, a Next.js interface, quantitative research routines and local agent integrations.

The public repository contains selected aggregate results, diagrams and methodology. The original implementation remains private to protect confidential data and operational material. No private research corpus or account credentials are included here.
