Aegis aims to make disciplined investment intelligence accessible beyond institutions: read the world, form a testable view, measure the decision, retain what was learned.

The goal is a useful investment engine people can understand. The system is in development; its research is evaluated through recorded experiments and paper accounts.

The implementation joins market research, quantitative experiments, paper evaluation and persistent project memory. Its history begins in March 2026. The public repository is a curated account of that work; the original operational implementation is private.

Research collection, evidence processing and decision support are potential services in their own right. They share a practical foundation: source acquisition, reproducible processing, explicit uncertainty and a record that another session can inspect.
